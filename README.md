# FNP-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard

**1. Project Overview**

The FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard is a data-driven business intelligence project designed to analyze and monitor sales performance across multiple dimensions such as revenue, orders, customer spending, product categories, occasions, cities, months, and order days.

The primary objective of the project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through an interactive dashboard. The dashboard enables management and business users to quickly understand where revenue is being generated, which products and occasions perform best, when customers place more orders, and which locations contribute significantly to sales.

The dashboard also provides interactive filtering capabilities based on Order Date, Delivery Date, and Occasion, allowing users to analyze specific periods or customer occasions and make more focused business decisions.

**2. Key Performance Indicators**

The dashboard provides four major KPIs representing the overall business performance:

KPI	Value	Interpretation
Total Revenue	₹35,20,984	Overall revenue generated from the analyzed sales
Total Orders	1,000	Total number of orders
Order-to-Delivery Time	5.53	Average time taken to deliver an order
Average Customer Spending	₹3,520.98	Average revenue generated per order/customer transaction

The relationship between total revenue and total orders indicates an average spending level of approximately ₹3,520.98 per order, providing an important benchmark for evaluating customer purchasing behavior.

**3. Revenue by Occasion**

The Revenue by Occasion analysis shows significant differences in customer spending across various occasions.

The major observations are:

Anniversary generates the highest revenue among the individual occasions, at approximately ₹6.7 lakh.
Raksha Bandhan is another strong-performing occasion, generating approximately ₹6.3 lakh.
All Occasions contributes around ₹5.8 lakh.
Holi generates approximately ₹5.7 lakh.
Birthday contributes around ₹4 lakh.
Valentine's Day generates approximately ₹3.3 lakh.
Diwali records comparatively lower revenue of around ₹3.1 lakh.
Business Insight

The results indicate that relationship-oriented and gifting occasions such as anniversaries and Raksha Bandhan are major revenue drivers. This suggests that FNP can potentially increase revenue by strengthening personalized campaigns, gift bundles, and promotional offers around these high-performing occasions.

At the same time, lower-performing occasions such as Diwali and Valentine's Day should be investigated further to understand whether the issue is related to product assortment, pricing, marketing effectiveness, or customer demand.

Business Insight

The large variations in monthly revenue suggest that the business has a strong seasonal component.

February and August show significant revenue spikes, which may be associated with specific gifting occasions, promotional campaigns, or customer purchasing patterns.

This information is valuable for:

Seasonal inventory planning
Marketing budget allocation
Workforce planning
Delivery capacity management
Promotional campaign scheduling

Rather than treating every month equally, management can allocate more resources around historically high-performing periods.

**11. Major Business Findings**

Based on the dashboard, the key findings are:

1. Strong overall sales performance

The business generated approximately ₹35.21 lakh from 1,000 orders, with an average spending of approximately ₹3,520.98 per order.

2. Anniversary is a major revenue driver

Among the displayed individual occasions, Anniversary generates the highest revenue.

3. Colors is the leading product category

The Colors category contributes approximately ₹10 lakh, making it the strongest category in the dashboard.

4. Seasonal demand is significant

Revenue varies considerably by month, with particularly strong performance in February and August.

5. Geographic demand is distributed

The top-order cities are spread across different regions, indicating opportunities for regional marketing and demand-based planning.

6. Tuesday and Sunday show stronger sales

These days generate substantially higher revenue than several other days, indicating differences in customer purchasing behavior.

7. Product revenue is diversified

The top five products each contribute meaningful revenue, reducing dependence on a single product.

12. Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies can help improve business performance:

1. Focus on high-performing occasions

Increase marketing investment around Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan, where customer spending is comparatively strong.

2. Strengthen high-revenue categories

Maintain inventory and expand product variations in categories such as Colors, Soft Toys, and Sweets.

3. Increase average order value

Create product bundles and cross-selling opportunities around the top-selling products to increase the current ₹3,520.98 average spending.

4. Use seasonal marketing

The significant monthly revenue fluctuations indicate that campaigns should be planned around historically strong periods rather than using a uniform marketing strategy throughout the year.

5. Develop city-specific strategies

Use order volume by city to identify high-potential markets and implement localized offers and targeted advertising.

6. Optimize delivery operations

Monitor the 5.53 average delivery time by city, occasion, and order period to identify opportunities for faster fulfillment.

7. Improve weaker segments

Lower-performing occasions and categories should not simply be discontinued. First, analyze customer demand, pricing, product assortment, and campaign performance before deciding on corrective action.

13. Overall Business Impact

The dashboard converts raw transaction-level sales information into a single decision-support system. Instead of manually examining large amounts of sales data, management can use the dashboard to quickly identify:

What is selling → Where it is selling → When customers are buying → Which occasions drive revenue → Which products perform best → How efficiently orders are delivered.

This enables data-driven decision-making in areas such as marketing, inventory management, sales planning, customer segmentation, product strategy, and delivery optimization.
